# Yawning Portal and Skullport

Version 0.4.0 adds four selectable Cyren maps:

| Location | Scene | Encounter groups | Intended Tokens |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Yawning Portal | `The Yawning Portal` | 1 | 1 |
| Skullport lower and middle levels | `Skullport - Lower & Middle` | 16 | 55 |
| Skull Island | `Skullport - Skull Island` | 7 | 145 |
| Skullport upper level | `Skullport - Upper` | 6 | 14 |
| **Total** | **4 maps** | **30** | **215** |

The Skullport datasets contain minimal placement metadata derived from the same private reference workflow used for Levels 3–23. They do not include adventure prose, stat blocks, journals, maps, or artwork.

The Yawning Portal dataset intentionally creates only Durnan. The adventure establishes him as the proprietor, and his map placement behind the bar is explicitly marked for human review because the Cyren Scene has no numbered area tile. General tavern patrons are not added because the source does not define a fixed static roster for them.

Use **Create missing safety copies** before testing. The helper creates `The Yawning Portal (Copy)` and one `(Copy)` Scene for each Skullport map when those copies do not already exist. Right-click the intended copy and choose **View Scene** before previewing or populating it.
